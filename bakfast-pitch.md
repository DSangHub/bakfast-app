# Bakfast.app — One-Page Pitch

**Returns without the runaround.**  
Customers hand returns to the driver delivering their next package (or schedule a pickup). Driver scans the Bakfast tag. Refund hits the next day.

---

## For Customers
- No more driving to the Post Office, FedEx, or UPS drop boxes.
- Hand the package to the driver on your next delivery, or schedule a dedicated pickup.
- Premium ($49.95/mo) includes SASE bags.
- Driver scans Bakfast tag → money transferred to your account the next day.
- Max 25 lb per package.
- Fraud protection: one misrepresentation or fraudulent transaction flags the address and delays the refund.
- Don’t miss the next sale waiting on a return.

**Pricing**  
- Basic: $19.95/month for 5 pickups  
- Premium: $49.95/month unlimited + SASE bags

## Customer Package Assurance
- Scan-to-scan protection while the package is with the Bakfast driver.
- Up to **$500 max payout** for loss or damage (simple limit for most returns).
- Verified drivers (linked to existing rideshare/delivery accounts).
- Fast claims process.
- Built to give customers confidence to sign up instead of driving to a drop box.

---

## For Drivers
- Extra earnings: $0.90 per mile.
- Pick up 10–15 packages a day.
- Create a profile and link existing rideshare or home-delivery accounts for verification.
- Choose specific merchants (Amazon, Walmart, etc.) or any pickup within a 10-mile radius.
- Color-coded map shows FedEx, Amazon, **UPS warehouses**, and member merchant return points — click to accept.
- Deliver returns to UPS (UPS accepts Amazon and many other retailer returns), then get paid by distance.
- Flexible side income using routes you already run.

---

## For Merchants
- Faster returns → higher customer satisfaction (CSI).
- Speedier processing and more buy clicks once the return is off the customer’s to-do list.
- Premium merchants get first response / priority pickup.
- Access to a verified user and address database built from Bakfast activity.
- Reduced friction increases repurchase rates and loyalty.
- Works alongside existing return labels and RMA processes.

---

## How It Works (3 Steps)
1. Customer requests pickup or hands package to next delivery driver (Premium members use SASE bags).
2. Driver scans the Bakfast tag (package ≤ 25 lb) and delivers the return to a UPS drop-off (UPS accepts Amazon and other retailer returns) or other designated hub.
3. Refund transfers to the customer’s account the next day (subject to fraud checks).

---

## Key Rules & Guardrails
- Maximum weight: 25 lb per package
- Driver compensation: $0.90 per mile
- Address flagging after one fraudulent or misrepresented transaction
- Driver verification via existing rideshare/delivery platform accounts
- Subscription model keeps the service sustainable for both sides

---

**Bakfast** turns the return into a reverse delivery that uses the drivers already on the road.  
Customers get convenience and speed. Drivers earn more. Merchants get better metrics and happier buyers.
